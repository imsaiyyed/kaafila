Delete your data

Kaafila uses anonymous accounts, so the quickest way is from the app on your phone.

In the app

  1. Open Kaafila.
  2. On the home screen, scroll down and tap Delete my data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This immediately:

  • removes you from your current journey, along with your location (if you lead the journey, it ends for everyone);
  • deletes your push token and your anonymous account;
  • clears your profile (name, car and colour) from the phone.

Stops, alerts and SOS messages you already sent stay visible to that journey's members until the journey is deleted, 3 days after it ends. Voice messages are deleted as soon as the journey ends.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email ismailsaiyyed60@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kaafila data" and the name and code of the journey you were in, and we will delete it within 7 days. Uninstalling the app also clears everything stored on the phone, and all journey data is deleted automatically: journeys end at the latest 24 hours after they start and are deleted 3 days after that.
