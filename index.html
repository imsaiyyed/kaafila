Kaafila

Walkie-talkie for cars travelling together

Driving to the same place in separate cars? Kaafila keeps the convoy in touch while Google Maps does the driving. No more phoning each car to plan a tea stop.

Get it on Google Play

Push-to-talk

Hold to talk. The message plays by itself in every other car, over your maps app.

One-tap stops

Tea, food, fuel. Everyone hears it and gets a Navigate button to your stop.

Gaps between cars

See who is ahead or behind, and hear a warning when a car falls too far back.

Quick alerts and SOS

"Slow down", "Take next exit" are read aloud. Hold SOS and every phone sounds an alarm.

Built for patchy highway signal: messages queue up and send when the signal returns. Your location is shared only with your convoy, only while a journey is active.
