Kaafila

Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Kaafila is a walkie-talkie app for cars travelling together. This policy explains what the app collects, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: ismailsaiyyed60@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your location and messages are shared only with the cars in the journey you joined, and only while that journey is active.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell or rent your data.
  • Locations and voice messages are deleted when the journey ends. Everything else about a journey is deleted 3 days later.
  • You can delete your data at any time from the app.

What Kaafila collects

Your profile. The name, car description and car colour you enter. They are stored on your phone and shown to the other members of journeys you join.

An anonymous account. The app signs in with a random ID so it can tell your phone apart from the others. There is no email, phone number or password.

Your precise location, only while you are in a journey. It includes your position, speed, heading and accuracy. It keeps being shared while Kaafila is in the background behind your maps app, which Android shows as an ongoing "Kaafila is running" notification. Only your latest position is kept.

Voice messages, recorded only while you hold the Talk button, and sent to the other cars in your journey.

Journey messages you send: stops, quick alerts and SOS, with your name, the time and your location at that moment.

A push token (Android), so the app can notify you about stops, alerts and SOS when it is closed.

The camera is used only to scan a journey's QR code on your phone; no pictures are stored or sent.

Kaafila asks for location, microphone and notification access only after explaining why, and you can refuse. Without location the others cannot see your car; without the microphone you cannot send voice messages.

How it is used and who sees it

This data is used only to run the app: showing each car on the map, working out who is ahead or behind, playing voice messages, announcing stops and alerts, and raising SOS alarms. It is visible only to the members of the same journey. Anyone who has a journey's code can join it, so share codes only with your group.

Services we use

Kaafila runs on Google Firebase (Realtime Database in Singapore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging and Cloud Functions) and shows maps with the Google Maps SDK. Google processes data for us under its own terms: see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. All data is encrypted in transit.

How long it is kept

  • A journey ends when its leader ends it, or automatically 24 hours after it was created.
  • When a journey ends, all locations and voice messages in it are deleted.
  • 3 days after a journey ends, everything else about it is deleted: members, names, stops, alerts and SOS messages.
  • Your push token is kept until you delete your data or uninstall the app.
  • Your profile stays on your phone until you delete your data or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap the gear on the home screen to open Settings, then tap Delete my data. More about deleting your data.

Children

Kaafila is meant for adults travelling together and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the change affects what the app shares, the app will explain it again before continuing.
